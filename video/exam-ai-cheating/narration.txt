One wrong answer. Out of one hundred sixty.
That was the first-round score of this year's top candidate on South Korea's tax accountant exam.

He says he did the same in both rounds of the tax exam, and in the first round of the CPA exam.
Police have seized the camera, and the government is reviewing this year's national certification exams.
