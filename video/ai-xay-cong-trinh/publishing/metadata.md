## Description (paste-ready)
Who really builds a building? Not just the crew with the hammers.

In under a minute, follow a construction project through its five stages: idea → design → approvals and tender → construction → inspection and handover. Along the way you meet the six parties behind every building:

• Owner: has the idea and receives the keys
• Design consultant: turns the idea into drawings
• QS (quantity surveyor): counts what every line costs
• Contractor: builds it
• Supervision consultant: checks every pour and every bolt against the approved drawings
• Government authority: approves the gates along the way

Why the lowest price is not always the best value, and why a project succeeds only when all six keep their promises to each other.

This is a simplified illustration of a typical process. It does not describe any specific project, law or regulation.
