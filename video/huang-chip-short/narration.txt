Nvidia's Jensen Huang just resigned. The headlines say: a reported affair, and a woman U.S. officials call a Chinese spy. But rewind.
In 2022, Washington banned his best AI chips from China. So Huang built a weaker chip for China. In 2025, that was banned too.
So he went to Trump, and made a deal: sell the chips, and give the government a cut. First fifteen percent. Then twenty-five.
China hawks hated it. Then, in October 2026, U.S. officials told the New York Times about the relationship and the spy claim. Beijing calls it fabrication. Two weeks later, he was out.
A real security risk? Or a dealmaker removed? The public has not seen the evidence. What do you think?
