# Short "Follow the chips" — kịch bản đề xuất (CHƯA DUYỆT)

## Lời dẫn đề xuất

1. **Hook.** Nvidia's Jensen Huang just resigned. The headlines say: a reported affair, and a woman U.S. officials call a Chinese spy. But rewind.
2. **Lệnh cấm.** In 2022, Washington banned his best AI chips from China. So Huang built a weaker chip for China. In 2025, that was banned too.
3. **Thỏa thuận.** So he went to Trump, and made a deal: sell the chips, and give the government a cut. First fifteen percent. Then twenty-five.
4. **Cú ngã.** China hawks hated it. Then, in October 2026, U.S. officials told the New York Times about the relationship and the spy claim. Beijing calls it fabrication. Two weeks later, he was out.
5. **Câu hỏi.** A real security risk? Or a dealmaker removed? The public has not seen the evidence. What do you think?
